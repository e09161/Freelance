Theme – Definition

NARROW‑MINDEDNESS

The quality or state of being unwilling or unready to accept ideas, beliefs, or behaviors that are different from your own.

IMAGINATION

The faculty or action of forming new ideas, or mental images or concepts of external objects not present to the senses.

Theme – Quotes

“They answered, ‘Why be scared of a hat?’ My drawing was not a picture of a hat. It was a picture of a boa constrictor digesting an elephant.”
“That's the crate. The sheep you want is inside.” … “That's exactly how I wanted it!”

Theme – Textual Evidence

🐍

The Boa Constrictor

Shows how adults are narrow‑minded because they only see a hat instead of imagining what the drawing represents.

📦

The Sheep Inside the Box

Shows how the Little Prince uses imagination to see beyond what is physically visible.

❤️

Seeing with the Heart

“It is only with the heart that one can see rightly; what is essential is invisible to the eye.”

Global Issue – Definition & Impact

RIGID SYSTEMS & SUPPRESSION

Rigid educational systems, suppression of creative thinking, and ideological extremism refer to situations where people are forced to follow strict rules, accept certain beliefs, and avoid questioning traditional ideas.

These situations discourage creativity, curiosity, and independent thinking.

Global Issue – Real Life

  • Some education systems focus so heavily on standardized testing that students never learn to think creatively.
  • Some governments censor art, literature, or ideas that challenge the status quo.
  • In some communities, expressing a different opinion can lead to social rejection or worse.

The conflict between narrow‑mindedness and imagination is not just a theme in a children's book — it is a real‑world challenge.

Reflection

STAY CURIOUS. QUESTION. IMAGINE.

The Little Prince reminds us to stay curious, question what we are told, and see beyond the surface. Like the Little Prince, we should trust our imagination and value what cannot be seen with the eyes alone.

Only then can we build a more open, creative, and compassionate world.
