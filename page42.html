Setting

🌍

Geographical

Mr. Zuckerman's farm (the barn & pigpen), the Arable family's house, and the County Fair Ground.

🌾

Environmental

The barn is warm, dusty and safe. The fair is loud and crowded. The mood is friendship, loyalty, hope and sadness.

🍂

Temporal

Spring to autumn, mid‑20th century. Clues: farm life, the county fair, no modern machines.

Why it matters: the barn is a safe little world where Wilbur is protected, but outside it his life is in danger. The fair is where Wilbur wins his prize — and where Charlotte becomes weak.

Theme – Definition

TEAMWORK

When a group works together, trusting each other, for one shared goal.

BETRAYAL

When one member turns against the group, or helps only for personal gain, putting the goal at risk.

Together: teamwork only works when everyone is loyal. One selfish member can break the trust and endanger the whole team.

Theme – Quotes

“I have no interest in saving pigs. I am a rat.” — TEMPLETON, CHAPTER 12: “A MEETING”
“Some Pig.” — WRITTEN IN CHARLOTTE'S WEB, CHAPTER 11: “THE MIRACLE”

Theme – Textual Evidence

🐀

1. Templeton's Refusal

He will only help if he gets food from Wilbur's trough. He puts himself before the team — that is betrayal.

🕸️

2. Charlotte's Meeting

She lets every animal share ideas to save Wilbur. This is the healthy side of teamwork: respect and democracy.

📰

3. Templeton Brings Words

He fetches words from the dump — but only because he benefits. Help that is selfish, not loyal.

Together, these three moments show that teamwork is fragile: it only works when everyone is willing to sacrifice.

Global Issue

PERSONAL AMBITION VS. TEAM SUCCESS

Definition: the struggle between pursuing your own goals or rewards and working with others for a shared purpose.

Connection to the theme: Templeton's betrayal comes directly from his personal ambition — he thinks about his stomach first.

Connection to the story: the animals work together to save Wilbur, but Templeton only helps when he is promised food.

Why it is global: it happens in schools, workplaces, sports teams and communities. When one person cares only about personal gain, teamwork breaks down — and everyone loses.

News Article Connection

“Sam Bankman‑Fried sentenced to 25 years for FTX fraud”

Source: BBC News  •  Date: 28 March 2024  •  Place: United States

  • What happened: the founder of the crypto company FTX stole billions from customers and investors to fund his own lifestyle. The company collapsed.
  • Who was affected: customers who lost their savings, employees who lost their jobs, and investors who lost their money.
  • Link to my global issue: like Templeton, he only acted when it benefited him personally — and the whole team suffered.
  • Lesson: personal ambition must be balanced with responsibility to others.

Literary Device – Foreshadowing

FORESHADOWING

Clues the author gives about what will happen later in the story.

“Charlotte heard it and knew that she hadn't much time left.” — CHAPTER 15: “THE CRICKETS”

Why it is foreshadowing: the line hints that Charlotte is running out of time, so readers predict she will soon die.

Effect on the reader: it creates a sad, tense mood, builds suspense, and prepares us emotionally for her death — making Charlotte's sacrifice feel even more urgent.

Reflection

BE LOYAL. WORK TOGETHER. KEEP YOUR PROMISES.

Charlotte's Web is much more than a story about animals. Through its setting, its theme of teamwork versus betrayal, its connection to a real global issue, and its use of foreshadowing, E. B. White teaches us that trust and sacrifice are what hold a team together — and that when one person puts himself first, everyone can lose.
