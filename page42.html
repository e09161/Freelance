Setting

🌍

Geographical

USA – Arable's barn → Mr. Zuckerman's barn → the County Fair Ground.

🕰️

Temporal

Mid‑20th century. Clues: farm traditions, importance of barns, rise of Fair Grounds.

🌾

Environmental

A loyal spider · beautiful friendship · ambitious rat · smelly barn · loud fair · a little sadness.

Why it matters: the barn is a safe world where Wilbur is protected; outside it his life is in danger. The fair is where Wilbur wins — but where Charlotte sacrifices herself.

Theme

TEAMWORK VS. BETRAYAL
TEAMWORK

A group works together to reach a shared goal. In the book, the goal is saving Wilbur's life.

BETRAYAL

A person takes advantage of loyalty and friendship to take everything only for himself.

Quote

“Now I called this meeting in order to get suggestions. I need new ideas for the web.” — CHARLOTTE, CHAPTER 12: “A MEETING”

Why it shows the theme: Charlotte, the leader, trusts her team enough to ask for their ideas. That is the positive side of teamwork — respect and democracy.

Textual Evidence

🐀

1. Templeton's Help

As a rat, he can reach the dump. He finds words like “Radiant” — essential because the other animals can't reach the trash.

🕸️

2. Charlotte's Democracy

She respects every animal's idea: “That's a good one — we can improve it.” She listens before deciding.

💔

3. Templeton's Betrayal

At the fair, he refuses to help unless promised Wilbur's food. Help with a selfish motive — a betrayal.

Together, these three moments show that teamwork is fragile: it only works when every member sacrifices.

Global Issue

CONFLICT BETWEEN PERSONAL AMBITION & TEAM SUCCESS

Definition: the struggle between pursuing personal rewards and working together for a shared purpose.

In the book: Templeton helps not out of friendship but because Charlotte promised him the used web to eat. His motive is personal.

Why it matters: when one person keeps everything the team worked for, trust breaks and the whole group loses.

Global Issue in Our Lives

“Bankman‑Fried sentenced to 25 years in prison for massive FTX fraud”

Source: PBS NewsHour  •  Place: United States  •  Year: 2024  •  Impact: global

  • What happened: FTX was a crypto exchange — an online “bank” the public trusted. Sam Bankman‑Fried stole customers' money and tried to disappear.
  • Who was affected: customers from many countries whose savings were stolen.
  • Link to my global issue: like Templeton, he put personal ambition before his team — company, employees, and customers.
  • Lesson: personal ambition must be balanced with responsibility.

Literary Device – Foreshadowing

FORESHADOWING

Clues the author gives about what will happen later in the story.

“Charlotte heard it and knew that she hadn't much time left.” — CHAPTER 15: “THE CRICKETS”

Effect on the reader: creates a sad, tense mood, builds suspense, and prepares us emotionally for Charlotte's death — making her sacrifice even more powerful.
