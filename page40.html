Theme – Definition

ABUSE OF POWER

Abuse of power happens when someone uses their authority to control, intimidate, or harm others.

In Matilda, Miss Trunchbull and Mr. Wormwood both misuse their power, but Trunchbull is the clearest example.

Theme – Quotes

"I'm right and you're wrong, I'm big and you're small, and there's nothing you can do about it."
– Mr. Wormwood (Abuse of Power)
"Miss Trunchbull, the Headmistress, was something else altogether. She was a gigantic holy terror, a fierce tyrannical monster who frightened the life out of the pupils and teachers alike."
– Narrator (Describing the Tyrant)

Both quotes show how power is misused through fear and intimidation.

Theme – Textual Evidence

🚪

The Chokey

Miss Trunchbull locks children in a small cupboard with nails and broken glass. This is physical and psychological abuse.

💇‍♀️

The Pigtail Incident

Trunchbull grabs Amanda Thripp by her pigtails and swings her around, abusing her authority and violating the child’s right to safety.

🎂

The Cake Incident

She force-feeds Bruce Bogtrotter an entire chocolate cake in front of the whole school, a form of public humiliation and cruel punishment.

Each example proves that Miss Trunchbull’s power is used to harm, not to help.

Global Issue – Definition & Impact

HUMAN RIGHTS' VIOLATIONS

A human rights violation occurs when a state or non-state actor purposefully ignores, denies, or infringes upon the fundamental rights and freedoms to which every person is entitled simply by being human.

Impact: children lose their safety, dignity, and right to learn in a secure environment – just like the students at Crunchem Hall.

Human Rights' Violations in Real Life

  • Corporal punishment: Some schools still allow teachers to physically hit students, violating children’s rights.
  • Emotional abuse: Children may face humiliation, fear, or systems that ignore their needs, which also violates their dignity.

UNICEF and international laws, like the Convention on the Rights of the Child, work to protect children from these abuses.

Reflection

WHAT CAN WE DO WHEN WE SEE ABUSE OF POWER?

Matilda teaches us to stand up against unfair authority and to speak for those who cannot speak for themselves.

Just like Matilda used her courage and intelligence, we can choose to challenge cruelty and protect human dignity.
