🎯 Exam-Day Strategy & Key Tips

📖 Reading Part 7 – Gapped Text (Mathias's priority)

Read the text before and after each gap carefully. Look for pronoun references, lexical cohesion, and logical connectors. The missing paragraph must fit both the preceding and following sentences. Practise with all Gold CD-ROM gapped text exercises on Canvas.

✍️ Writing – Essay (Part 1)

Always address two of the three bullet points. State your opinion clearly in the introduction. Use a range of C1 linking devices. Keep to 220–260 words.

🔄 Use of English Part 4 – Key Word Transformations

Never change the key word. Write 3–6 words. Contractions count as two words. Practise daily – this is the most formulaic and improvable part.
