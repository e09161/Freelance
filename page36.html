💻 Digital vs. Paper-based

All four main centers offer both formats. Here's how to choose.

🖥️ Digital (Computer-based)

  • Faster results — typically 2–3 weeks
  • Multiple test dates available per month at some centers
  • Headphones for Listening (better audio isolation)
  • Typing for Writing (advantage if you type faster than you write)
  • On-screen timer and word count tools
  • Same exam content and difficulty as paper
Recommended if you're comfortable with computers

📄 Paper-based

  • Results in 4–6 weeks
  • Familiar format for those who prefer writing by hand
  • Can annotate and circle text directly on the paper
  • Listening played on loudspeaker (room acoustics matter)
  • Limited test dates (only on official global dates)
  • Same exam content and difficulty as digital
Choose if you prefer paper reading & handwriting
💡 Which is better? There is no advantage in scoring — both are accepted identically by universities and employers. Choose based on your comfort with the medium. If you type essays for practice, choose Digital. If you annotate heavily when reading, choose Paper-based.

📝 Registration & Practical Info

What to prepare before you register, and what to expect on test day.

📋 Before You Register

  • Valid ID: Passport or DNI (check center requirements)
  • Payment method: Most centers accept card or bank transfer
  • Preferred date: Have 2–3 options ready in case one is full
  • Preferred format: Digital or Paper-based
  • Speaking availability: Confirm the speaking window

📌 Registration Timeline

  • 6–8 weeks before: Ideal registration window for December dates
  • 4 weeks before: Last reasonable window; prices may rise
  • 2 weeks before: Very limited availability; risk of full sessions
  • Late registration: Some centers offer it with a surcharge

🎒 On Test Day — Bring With You

  • ✅ Valid ID matching your registration name exactly
  • ✅ Printed confirmation email (or digital copy)
  • ✅ Pencil, eraser, and blue/black pen (paper-based only)
  • ✅ Water bottle (transparent, no label)
  • ❌ No phones, smartwatches, or study materials in the exam room
⚠️ Name consistency: The name on your ID must match the name you use to register. If not, you may be denied entry. Use your passport name if you plan to use a passport as your ID.

🧭 How to Choose Your Center

Five factors to weigh before making your final decision.

📍 Location & Commute

Pick a center close to home or with a reliable route. Arriving stressed after a long commute can affect your performance, especially for Speaking.

💻 Format Availability

Not every center offers both formats on the exact date you want. Confirm the format availability before committing.

💰 Total Cost

Compare prices, but also consider travel cost and time. The Británico at S/ 790 is the lowest starting price, but other factors may override this.

⏳ Result Delivery

Digital format typically delivers results in 2–3 weeks vs. 4–6 weeks for Paper-based. If you need results urgently, choose Digital.

🗣️ Speaking Window

Ask the center when the Speaking portion will be scheduled. Some centers confirm it at registration; others announce it later.

📞 Responsiveness

Call or email the center before registering. Prompt, clear answers indicate good administrative support — important if issues arise.
