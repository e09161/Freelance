Official test dates, authorized centers, formats, and pricing summary. November/December 2026 & January 2027.

📅 Global Official Test Dates

Cambridge English sets the following central dates. Lima test centers schedule their sessions on or around these dates.

  • 📆 December 5, 2026 (Saturday)
  • 📆 December 12, 2026 (Saturday)
  • 📆 January 23, 2027 (Saturday)
🗣️ Speaking test note: The Speaking portion is scheduled independently by each local center, usually within a few days before or after the main written date.

💡 Registration Tips

  • Internal vs. external: If you are a current student at Británico, PUCP, or Universidad del Pacífico, you may receive a discount on the exam fee.
  • Digital vs. paper-based: Both formats are equally accepted. Digital results may be available slightly faster.
  • Speaking test date: You will be notified of your individual speaking time after registration; plan your schedule accordingly.
  • Early registration: Centers have limited capacity; register at least 6–8 weeks before the written date.
